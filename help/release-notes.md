# [!DNL Adobe Marketo Qualifier] release notes

## Upcoming release

* Find and prioritize the right accounts and leads with Smart audience (Beta). Describe the accounts you want in plain language, add CRM data and growth signals, review scored leads, and add them to an Outbound Workflow. [Learn more](smart-audience.md).
* Filter contacts by account fields, alongside contact and opportunity fields, to find the right contacts and target Outbound Workflows more precisely. [Learn more](prospects.md#filter-and-find-prospects).
* Contacts show their associated account and owner name, and a prospect's page now shows 90 days of marketing activity, up from 30. [Learn more](prospects.md#review-prospect-details).

## 10-08-2026

This release includes:

* Reuse an Outbound Workflow or change the touchpoints of a running workflow, duplicate it and edit the copy. The copy starts as a private draft with no prospects, and the original workflow is unchanged. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#duplicate-an-outbound-workflow).
* Configure Outbound Workflows in a compact layout with navigation between the goal, targeting, touchpoints, and settings. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#create-an-outbound-workflow).
* Clear opt-out requests in email replies are recognized automatically. The prospect is marked as opted out, and no further touchpoints are sent. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#opt-out-requests-in-replies).
* Prepare for meetings with a single brief that combines web research and product-specific customer case studies, with talking points ranked by relevance and sources identified. [Learn more](prospects.md#generate-meeting-prep).
* Generated emails are checked against quality guidelines and rewritten when needed before you review them. You still review, edit, and approve every email before it is sent. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#review-and-refine-generated-emails).

## 09-22-2026

This release includes:

* View [!DNL Marketo Sales Insights] and agentic data in the embedded CRM plugin, and add prospects to [!DNL Marketo Qualifier]. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#crm-mcp-and-the-embedded-plugin).
* Prioritize CRM leads with Best Bets, My Watch List, web activity, email engagement, and webinar activity. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#prioritize-leads-in-the-crm-plugin).
* Automatically enroll prospects in Outbound Workflows when their live [!DNL Marketo] activity matches the workflow criteria. [Learn more](home.md#automatically-enroll-prospects-from-marketing-highlights).
* Review prospect context and export the prospect list, and view AI person summaries on [!DNL Marketo Optimizer] Prime and Ultimate instances. [Learn more](prospects.md#review-prospect-context-and-export-the-list).
* Review and approve generated prospect emails from the task queue, and reply with agent suggestions during an Outbound Workflow. [Learn more](tasks.md).

## 09-08-2026

This release includes:

* Set your own email drafting style once in Profile settings, and every generated email follows it. [Learn more](profile-settings.md#email-drafting-context).
* Generate goal-based or custom meeting prep from a prospect's page, in a new Meeting research tab. [Learn more](prospects.md#generate-meeting-prep).
* Admins can assign an Outbound Workflow to a teammate and reset workflow settings to their defaults. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#create-an-outbound-workflow).
* Map custom fields when importing prospects from a CSV, and use those values in generated emails. [Learn more](prospects.md#build-your-prospect-list).
* Generated emails use any additional prospect data you import and can be written natively in the prospect's language. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#step-5-add-prospects-and-start-email-generation).
* Outbound Performance shows open rate and click rate by default, with a toggle for raw counts, and total prospects at the organization level. [Learn more](performance.md).
* CRM Sync Rules automatically update CRM status as a prospect moves through an Outbound Workflow. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#configure-crm-sync-rules).
* Ask AI Chat questions across your [!DNL Marketo Qualifier], CRM, [!DNL Marketo], and [!DNL Marketo Optimizer] data. [Learn more](ai-assistant.md#ask-ai-chat-across-your-connected-data).

## 08-17-2026

[!DNL Marketo Qualifier] is now available as a stand-alone app. It supports [!DNL Marketo Engage] and [!DNL Marketo Optimizer].

This release includes:

* Prospect and account prioritization with AI-generated activity summaries and signal-based scoring. [Learn more](prospects.md#review-prospect-details) about prospects or [accounts](accounts.md#account-insights).
* Goal-driven Outbound Workflows with AI-proposed cadences and drafted emails. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md).
* A unified task queue for phone calls, LinkedIn InMails, and email reviews. [Learn more](tasks.md).
* Automatic meeting booking through calendar integration. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#meeting-booking).
* A Knowledge Center for grounding AI outreach in your own playbook materials. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#knowledge-center).
* AI Chat for natural-language questions based on your CRM, engagement, and Knowledge Center data. [Learn more](ai-assistant.md).
* Email and meeting-booking performance reports. [Learn more](performance.md).
* Browser and email plugins for access within your CRM or Outlook. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#crm-mcp-and-the-embedded-plugin).
* Support for Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics 365 integrations. [Learn more](integrations.md).
