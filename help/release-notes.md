# Sales Qualifier Release Notes

## 09-08-2026

This release includes:

* Set your own email drafting style once in Profile settings, and every generated email follows it. [Learn more](profile-settings.md#email-drafting-context).
* Generate goal-based or custom meeting prep from a prospect's page, in a new Meeting research tab. [Learn more](prospects.md#generate-meeting-prep).
* Admins can assign an Outbound Workflow to a teammate and reset workflow settings to their defaults. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#create-an-outbound-workflow).
* Map custom fields when importing prospects from a CSV, and use those values in generated emails. [Learn more](prospects.md#build-your-prospect-list).
* Generated emails use any additional prospect data you import and can be written natively in the prospect's language. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#step-5-add-prospects-and-start-email-generation).
* Outbound Performance shows open rate and click rate by default, with a toggle for raw counts, and total prospects at the organization level. [Learn more](performance.md).
* CRM Sync Rules automatically update CRM status as a prospect moves through an Outbound Workflow. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#configure-crm-sync-rules).
* Ask AI Chat questions across your Sales Qualifier, CRM, [!DNL Marketo], and [!DNL Adobe Journey Optimizer B2B Edition] data. [Learn more](ai-assistant.md#ask-ai-chat-across-your-connected-data).

## 08-17-2026

[!DNL Sales Qualifier] is now available as a stand-alone application. It supports Marketo and Adobe Journey Optimizer B2B.

This release includes:

* Prospect and account prioritization with AI-generated activity summaries and signal-based scoring. [Learn more](prospects.md#review-prospect-details) about prospects or [accounts](accounts.md#account-insights).
* Goal-driven Outbound Workflows with AI-proposed cadences and drafted emails. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md).
* A unified task queue for phone calls, LinkedIn InMails, and email reviews. [Learn more](tasks.md).
* Automatic meeting booking through calendar integration. [Learn more](outbound-workflows.md#meeting-booking).
* A Knowledge Center for grounding AI outreach in your own playbook materials. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#knowledge-center).
* AI Chat for natural-language questions based on your CRM, engagement, and Knowledge Center data. [Learn more](ai-assistant.md).
* Email and meeting-booking performance reports. [Learn more](performance.md).
* Browser and email plugins for access within your CRM or Outlook. [Learn more](admin-settings.md#crm-mcp-and-the-embedded-plugin).
* Support for Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics 365 integrations. [Learn more](integrations.md).
