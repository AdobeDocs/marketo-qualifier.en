# Tasks

Use **[!UICONTROL Tasks]** to complete the actions generated by Outbound Workflows. Select a task, take action, mark the task complete, and continue to the next task without leaving the page.

In the left navigation, go to **[!UICONTROL Activities]** > **[!UICONTROL Tasks]**.

![Task panel](assets/tasks.png)

## Task views

The page has two tabs:

* **[!UICONTROL Manual tasks]**—Phone calls, LinkedIn InMails, and email reviews for prospects who are enrolled in an Outbound Workflow.
* **[!UICONTROL Agent suggestions]**—Prospects that match an Outbound Workflow's targeting criteria and are recommended for enrollment.

Each tab has its own filters, sorting options, and two-panel layout. The task list appears on the left, and the work panel appears on the right. Selecting a task loads its details in the work panel. When you complete a task, the next task is selected automatically.

## Manual tasks

### Task types

Manual tasks are tied to Outbound Workflow steps and come in three types:

* **[!UICONTROL Phone Call]**—Created when a cadence reaches a phone call step. The work panel shows the prospect's phone number and, when available, an AI-generated call script.

* **[!UICONTROL LinkedIn InMail]**—Created when a cadence reaches a LinkedIn InMail step. The work panel shows content to copy and send from LinkedIn. Expand **[!UICONTROL AI Rationale]** to review the rationale.

* **[!UICONTROL Email Review]**—Created after Adobe Marketo Qualifier generates a prospect's personalized emails. Select **[!UICONTROL Review emails]** to review and approve the drafts before outreach begins. See [Review and refine generated emails](outbound-workflows.md#review-and-refine-generated-emails).

![Email Review](assets/tasks-review.png)

### The work panel

For a **[!UICONTROL Phone Call]** or **[!UICONTROL LinkedIn InMail]** task, the work panel contains:

* **[!UICONTROL Prospect]**—The prospect's name, email link, and phone number, when applicable.
* **[!UICONTROL Outbound Workflow]**—The linked Outbound Workflow name, due date, and auto-skip indicator, when applicable.
* **Task content**—The call script or InMail content.
* **[!UICONTROL Notes]**—Notes save automatically when you select another task. You cannot edit notes after a task is completed, skipped, or canceled.

### Generate a call script

For a **[!UICONTROL Phone Call]** task, select **[!UICONTROL Generate call script]**. When generation finishes, select **[!UICONTROL View Detailed Call Script]**. If generation fails, try again from the panel.

### Task actions

Two actions are available from the work panel header:

* **[!UICONTROL Mark complete]**—Use this action after you make the call, send the InMail, or review the emails. The queue advances to the next task.
* **[!UICONTROL Skip]**—Use this action when you cannot complete the step but want to keep the prospect in the Outbound Workflow. The prospect advances to the next cadence step.

Phone call and LinkedIn InMail tasks can be skipped automatically if they remain open past the configured threshold. An automatic skip advances the prospect through the cadence and does not affect scheduled email touchpoints.

### Filter, search, and sort

The toolbar above the list controls which tasks appear and in what order. Your filter and sort choices are saved and reapplied the next time you open the page.

* **[!UICONTROL Filter]**—Open the filter panel:
  * **[!UICONTROL Status]**—**[!UICONTROL Current]**, **[!UICONTROL Upcoming]**, **[!UICONTROL Overdue]**, **[!UICONTROL Completed]**, **[!UICONTROL Cancelled]**, **[!UICONTROL Skipped]**.
  * **[!UICONTROL Task type]**—**[!UICONTROL Email Review]**, **[!UICONTROL LinkedIn InMail]**, **[!UICONTROL Phone Call]**.
  * **[!UICONTROL Due date]**.
  * **[!UICONTROL Outbound Workflow]**—A searchable list of your Outbound Workflows.
* **[!UICONTROL Sort]**—Sort by due date or creation date. The sort order also determines the order in which the queue advances.
* **[!UICONTROL Search tasks]**—Find tasks by prospect name, company name, or Outbound Workflow. Search applies with active filters.

Active filters appear as chips below the toolbar. Select **[!UICONTROL Clear all]** to reset them.

### Task status

Each task shows its current status:

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **[!UICONTROL Current]** | Due now and ready to act on. Current tasks show no badge. |
| **[!UICONTROL Upcoming]** | The preceding step is complete, but the due date is in the future. You can act early if the moment is right. |
| **[!UICONTROL Overdue]** | Past the due date and not yet completed. The task is flagged for attention. |
| **[!UICONTROL Completed]** | You completed the action and marked the task complete. |
| **[!UICONTROL Skipped]** | You skipped the step, or it skipped automatically. The prospect advances in the Outbound Workflow. |
| **[!UICONTROL Cancelled]** | The system canceled the task because of an Outbound Workflow change. |

Completed, skipped, and canceled tasks are final. Their actions are no longer available, and their notes are read-only.

## Agent suggestions

The **[!UICONTROL Agent suggestions]** tab lists prospects that match an Outbound Workflow's targeting criteria and are recommended for enrollment. To turn on recommendations, see [Outbound Workflows](outbound-workflows.md).

Select a suggestion to review it in the work panel:

* A recency badge marks each suggestion as **[!UICONTROL New]** or **[!UICONTROL Previous]**.
* The **[!UICONTROL Recommended leads]** or **[!UICONTROL Recommended contacts]** table lists the proposed prospects with columns for **[!UICONTROL Name]**, **[!UICONTROL Title]**, **[!UICONTROL Account]**, **[!UICONTROL Status]**, **[!UICONTROL Email]**, and **[!UICONTROL Last updated]**.

Two actions are available:

* **[!UICONTROL Review prospects]**—Open the Outbound Workflow to review and enroll recommended prospects. See [Add prospects and start email generation](outbound-workflows.md#step-5-add-prospects-and-start-email-generation).
* **[!UICONTROL Mark as complete]**—Dismiss the suggestion after you review it.

The **[!UICONTROL Agent suggestions]** tab includes **[!UICONTROL Current]**, **[!UICONTROL Completed]**, and **[!UICONTROL Cancelled]** status filters, an Outbound Workflow filter, and sorting by creation date.

## Complete tasks from an Outbound Workflow

On an Outbound Workflow's **[!UICONTROL Engaged Prospects]** view, a manual touchpoint provides the same **[!UICONTROL Mark as complete]**, **[!UICONTROL Skip]**, and notes options. Completing a task there also updates its status on the **[!UICONTROL Tasks]** page. See [Outbound Workflows](outbound-workflows.md).

## Empty states

* When you have no tasks to act on, the list shows a _You're all caught up for today_ message.
* When filters match no tasks, the list reports that no tasks match your filters.
* When no task is selected, the work panel prompts you to select a task to view its details.
